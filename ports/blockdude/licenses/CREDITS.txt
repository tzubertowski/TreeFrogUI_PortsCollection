Blockdude for the Data Frog SF3500 - credits and licences
==========================================================

Game code
  Blockdude (funkey version) - (c) 2006-2024 Willems Davy (joyrider3774, "Willems Soft").
  MIT licence, text in blockdude-funkey-LICENSE.txt.
  Source: https://github.com/joyrider3774/blockdude_funkey
  The SF3500 changes (display, audio and pad glue, on-screen keyboard, parts palette, picture
  tuning) are in the sf3500 branch of https://github.com/Kryptikone316/blockdude_funkey and are
  under the same MIT licence.

Game concept and levels
  Blockdude is a remake of the TI-83 game by Brandon Sterner.
  The "blockman" level pack comes from Blockman by Soleau Software (http://www.soleau.com).
  The Blockdude author states that he had Soleau Software's approval to use the levels. The
  permission was given to him, not to this port; we have not been able to verify it independently.

Graphics (blockdudefs/graphics, blockdudefs/skins)
  Default skin: Chris Katiesen, made for Klas Kroon's remake of Blockdude/Blockman
  (http://oos.moxiecode.com). Used with permission obtained by the Blockdude author.
  FP_Tech and FP_Default_2.0 skins: Fusion Power. In this port's copy of the FP_Tech title screen
  the first four pixel columns were repaired (the stem of the letter B, which the original image
  clips at its left edge); nothing else was changed.
  Ti-83 skin: after Brandon Sterner's TI-83 game.
  No separate licence text for the graphics ships with the upstream project.

Cyberdeck skin (blockdudefs/skins/Cyberdeck)
  Original pixel art made for this port, drawn by a script (tools/make_cyberdeck.py in the
  source repository); same MIT licence as the game. Fonts: DejaVu Sans Mono, see above.

Music and sound
  Title music (blockdudefs/music/title.ogg): donskeeto. The licence is not stated upstream.
  Sound effects (blockdudefs/sound/*.wav): the origin is not stated upstream.

Fonts (blockdudefs/fonts)
  font.ttf = DejaVu Sans, font2.ttf = DejaVu Sans Condensed, font1.ttf = DejaVu Sans Mono.
  (c) Bitstream, Inc. (Bitstream Vera) and Tavmjong Bah (Arev glyphs); DejaVu changes are in the
  public domain. Licence text in DejaVu-fonts-LICENSE.txt. These replace the original game's
  Verdana, IrisUPC and Monofonto fonts, which are not free to redistribute.
