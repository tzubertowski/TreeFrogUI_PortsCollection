Defense Mania for the Data Frog SF3500 - credits and licences
=============================================================

Game
  Defense Mania - Kris Siepert (kriskbx). MIT licence, text in defense-mania-LICENSE.txt (the
  original licence file names no copyright holder; the year is 2015).
  Source: https://github.com/DefenseMania/defense-mania
  Graphics: https://github.com/DefenseMania/defense-mania-graphics (MIT licence, same text).
  The SF3500 port (native C++ translation, display, audio and pad glue, extra maps, difficulty
  progression, endless mode, scores, the Bastion and the other additions below) is in the sf3500 branch of
  https://github.com/Kryptikone316/defense-mania and is under the same MIT licence.
  The display, audio and pad glue is derived from TreeFrogUI's pico286 SF3000 patch.

Maps
  Mountains, The Maze, Dark Woods, River Side and Bigger are the original game's (The Maze has a
  northern loop that creatures may now walk). The Swamp, The Tundra and The Coast were made for this
  port from the original's tiles and procedural art (tools/make_swamp.py, tools/make_tundra.py and
  tools/make_coast.py in the source repository); same MIT licence.

Made for this port (same MIT licence)
  The Bastion and its research, actions and doctrines; the soldiers (their models are rendered in
  Blender by tools/blender/ in the source repository); the path traps; the Resupply Depot; the new
  creatures (recoloured from the original's sheets); the per-map HUD planks and the gold icon.

Sound effects (data/sfx)
  Synthesised by tools/make_sfx.py in the source repository. CC0.

  stb_image-LICENSE.txt.

Music (optional pack, not included)
  Sixteen Ogg Vorbis tracks, arranged by the port author from free instruments and samples and

  carries the text.
